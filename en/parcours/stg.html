    Hôtel Foyot and Hôtel Royal Condé33, rue de Tournon et 10, rue de Condé, 75006 Paris · Metro Odéon (4, 10)GoneLodging1927–1928

    From late 1927 to May 1928 he lives in room 21 of the Hôtel Foyot, facing the Senate: Gerald Yorke comes to see him here, Kasimira Bass arrives on 5 March, and on 22 March he recovers the rights to The Diary of a Drug Fiend. He spends the summer of 1928 at the nearby Hôtel Royal Condé. The Foyot was demolished in the late 1930s.
