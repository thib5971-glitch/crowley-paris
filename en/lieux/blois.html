The story

The Hôtel de Blois was a modest furnished hotel on rue Vavin, a stone's throw from the crossroads of the same name and the cafés of Montparnasse, in a street that also acquired, in 1912-1914, the stepped apartment building by Henri Sauvage and Charles Sarazin at no. 26. Its owner, M. Bourcier, often let Crowley stay on credit. The Aleister Crowley Timeline gives 49 rue Vavin for 1908, but Tobias Churton places the hotel at no. 50 from that year on, notably during the magical retreat of October 1908.

Crowley moved in on 28 April 1908, as his marriage to Rose Kelly was falling apart (the separation was only decided after a return to England), and spent the summer working there on several texts before leaving on 31 July with Victor Neuburg for a walking tour in Spain. He returned in 1911 (from 30 April to 3 May, then in August) and stayed there on 30 January 1914, during the “Paris Working”. This series of sex-magick operations carried out with Neuburg began on the night of 31 December 1913 with an invocation of Mercury (Thoth) and ended on 12 February 1914 with the twenty-fourth; Jupiter was the other principal deity, and Walter Duranty took part. The published text does not say where the sessions were held, some of which took place at friends' homes such as Jane Chéron's: the hotel is therefore the probable, not certain, setting of the whole.

Back from Cefalù, Crowley stayed there from late January to 31 March 1921; there, on 11 March, he initiated the writer Mary Butts into the first degree of the O.T.O. In 1924 he lived there from 15 January to 1 May, ill and penniless: on 8 March he pawned everything to send Norman Mudd to London, received Nina Hamnett, and Leah Hirsig joined him there on 29 March. On 1 May both were thrown out for unpaid rent; Crowley, who saw it as a “birth”, moved, according to Churton, to a cheap room in an inn at Chelles-sur-Marne and had his mail sent poste restante on rue Littré; the timeline then records Leah at 6 rue Jolia.

The sources do not say what became of the Hôtel de Blois. A hotel, the Hôtel Atelier Vavin, exists today at 49 rue Vavin, but no document establishes that it is the same establishment.

To be checked · Churton gives 50 rue Vavin from 1908 on; the timeline's 49 is probably a mistake.
